About us

Brisbane Plastics Company is a Queensland-owned manufacturer. From 12 October 2026 we own and run Plastics Unique and Sov Services, a Brisbane plastics fabricator trusted by local businesses for 27 years.

What the change of ownership means for you

We are engineers who spent decades inside manufacturing businesses before deciding to own one. We bought Plastics Unique because it makes good products for loyal customers. Our job is to keep that going and make it easier to do business with us.

  • Same factory. Production stays in Tingalpa.
  • Same products. Your existing products and specifications carry over.
  • Same way to order. Call, email or use the quote form on this site.
  • Better tools over time. Faster quotes, online artwork approval and simple repeat ordering are on the way.

Our directors

MB

Mathew Bowden

Managing Director

Mathew runs the business day to day. He is a controls and automation engineer who founded Alertify in 2017, building industrial IoT monitoring devices in Queensland, and grew it into a manufacturing consultancy: SCADA systems, PLC programming, line commissioning and AI control algorithms for clients from defence to soft drink manufacturers.

In 2024 he co-founded Engram AI Solutions, building AI tools for industrial use. At Brisbane Plastics Company his focus is the factory floor: production flow, quality, maintenance and making sure every job goes out right.

BB

Bruce Bairstow

Director

Bruce spent 20 years building a project management consultancy, LogiKal Projects, growing it to more than 100 consultants across Australia, Asia and the UK before selling it in 2022. His work spans major infrastructure and resources projects and managing complex programmes.

At Brisbane Plastics Company he leads strategy, systems and investment, and finds the commercial opportunity inside the operational challenge.

Part of FormFill Group

Brisbane Plastics Company is part of FormFill Group, an engineer-led group that buys and improves South East Queensland plastics manufacturers.

  • Owner-operated. The founders run the businesses themselves, alongside their investors.
  • Engineering depth. Controls, automation and IoT experience applied inside our own factories.
  • Local. Queensland-owned, Australian-made.

Where we are taking it

We are connecting the customer, the office and the factory so every order is entered once, artwork is approved once, and every job makes the next one easier to reorder.

  1. Send your brief

    Product, size, material, quantity and any artwork. The quote form asks for everything we need, so we get it right first time.

  2. Get your quote

    Standard products are quoted quickly. Custom jobs are reviewed by our team before pricing.

  3. Approve the artwork

    You approve a proof before anything is printed or cut. Nothing goes to the factory floor without your sign-off.

  4. Made and delivered

    Cut, printed, welded and assembled in Tingalpa, checked and dispatched or ready for pick-up.
