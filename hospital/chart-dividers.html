Questions we get asked

Is there a minimum order?

There is no minimum for the standard blank and printed 5-tab dividers. Custom tab dividers have a minimum of 50 sets.

What if we need more than seven tabs?

With eight or more tabs we suggest a set of five in front with the rest behind, so every tab stays readable in the binder.

Will they fit our binders?

The dividers are made for A4 2-ring (2D) binders, which most patient chart systems use. If yours differ, send us a sample or the ring spacing.
