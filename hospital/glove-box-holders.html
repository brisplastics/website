Questions we get asked

Can the holders be cleaned?

Yes. The wall bracket lets the holder lift off, so the holder and the wall behind it can be cleaned.

Do they fit all glove boxes?

The stainless steel spring-return clips grip standard glove and tissue boxes and adjust to the box size.

Is there a double holder?

Yes. Ask for the double holder on the order form and we will confirm availability and price.
