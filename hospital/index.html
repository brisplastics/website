Changes for existing Sov Services customers

Is Sov Services still trading?

Yes. Sov Services has supplied patient chart folders and holders to Australian hospitals since 1994. From 26 October 2026 it is owned and run by Brisbane Plastics Company from the same Tingalpa factory, with the same products and item codes.

Do your item codes stay the same?

Yes. Every SS- item code carries over, so you can reorder from your last order sheet or purchase order without changes.

Can we still email our orders?

Yes. support@sovservices.net.au stays open, and so does our phone number, 1300 744 104. The online order form is quicker because it asks for the ward, colour and print details we need, so we do not have to come back to you.

Has your ABN changed?

Yes. Orders from 26 October 2026 are supplied and invoiced by Brisbane Plastics Company Pty Ltd, ABN 14 699 248 658. If your purchasing system still lists the old supplier, please ask your supply team to update the vendor record. We will verify bank details by phone on request.

Can you print our ward name and bed numbers?

Yes. Folders can be printed with the ward name and bed number on the front and spine in black or white. Tell us the ward name and the bed numbers you need on the order form.
