Questions we get asked

Which spine size do I need?

Choose by how many pages the chart holds during an admission. Tell us roughly how many pages your charts carry and we will suggest a spine size.

What is the difference between generic and custom print?

Generic print has ward and bed fields on the front and spine for staff to fill in. Custom print has your ward name and the bed number printed for you. Custom print has a one-off digital artwork set-up charge.

Which colours work best with black print?

Yellow and grey give the strongest contrast with black print. White print works best on the darker colours such as Navy, Oxford Blue and Purple.

Can we order a single colour per ward?

Yes. Many hospitals colour-code by ward or unit. Tell us the colour code and quantity for each ward on one order.
