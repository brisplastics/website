Questions we get asked

What is the rollover?

The rollover is the curved top lip that hooks over the bed-end rail, so the chart sits at the foot of the bed without fixings.

Do the clipboards take a ring binder?

Each clipboard is fitted with either a standard clipboard clip or two D-ring clips. Tell us which on your order.

Can the A4 holder go on the wall?

Yes. The wall-mounted A4 holder comes in horizontal (SS-PCU-038) and vertical (SS-PCU-039) versions.
