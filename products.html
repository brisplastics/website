What we make

Every product is made to order in our Tingalpa factory. Tell us the size, material, quantity and print, and we will quote it.

  • Folders and ring binders

    PVC and polypropylene binders, presentation folders, document holders and manual covers, printed or foil-blocked with your brand.

  • Wallets, satchels and pockets

    Welded PVC wallets, zip satchels, clear pockets and notebook covers for dealerships, schools and offices.

  • Hospital products

    Patient chart holders, bed chart folders and ward stationery, supplied through our Sov Services division.

  • Mining and industrial

    Heavy-gauge covers, document and permit holders and site signage products built for hard conditions.

  • Packaging and custom work

    Specialised packaging and one-off fabricated products. Send us a drawing, a sample or a sketch.

  • Printing and branding

    Screen printing, flatbed UV printing and foil blocking, applied in-house so your job stays under one roof.

Materials

  • Flexible PVC. Clear, coloured and frosted sheet in a range of gauges. Welds cleanly for strong, sealed seams.
  • Polypropylene. Lightweight, rigid and durable. Ideal for folders, dividers and covers.
  • Heavy-gauge sheet. For mining, construction and industrial covers that need to last on site.

Processes

  • High-frequency (RF) welding. Seals PVC from the inside out for neat, strong seams on wallets, binders and pockets.
  • Cutting and die cutting. Cut to size and shape, from single samples to production runs.
  • Screen printing and UV flatbed printing. Spot colours by screen, full colour and white by UV flatbed up to 1250 × 2500 mm.
  • Foil blocking. Metallic branding on covers and binders.
  • Assembly. Ring mechanisms, eyelets, rivets, press studs and pen loops fitted in-house.
