Acrylic printing in Brisbane

Full-colour printing on clear, frosted and coloured acrylic, face printed, reverse printed behind clear, or with a white diffuser for backlit signs. Sheets up to 1250 × 2500 mm.

Get a quotePrinter specification

Acrylic print options

  • Face printed. Colour printed on the front surface. Good for opaque and coloured acrylic.
  • Reverse printed (second surface). Printed on the back of clear acrylic with a white backing, so the image sits behind a glossy, protected face.
  • Backlit. Colour with a white diffuser layer for light boxes and illuminated signs.
  • White only or spot white. Crisp white text and logos on clear, frosted or coloured acrylic.

Typical acrylic jobs

  • Reception and office wall signs
  • Door, room and wayfinding signs
  • Acrylic photo prints and wall art
  • Counter displays, stands and menu holders
  • Light box faces and backlit panels
  • Plaques, awards and donor boards
  • Splashbacks and decorative panels

Questions we get asked

What thickness of acrylic can you print?

Any thickness up to 50.8 mm. Most signs use 3 mm, 4.5 mm or 6 mm sheet.

Can you print behind clear acrylic?

Yes. Reverse (second-surface) printing puts the image behind the acrylic with a white backing, so it reads through a glossy, scratch-protected face.

Can you cut acrylic to shape?

We cut to size in house. Tell us the shape you need and we will quote it.
