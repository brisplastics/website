UV flatbed printing · Brisbane

Retail and point-of-sale display printing

Counter displays, menu boards, shelf signs and packaging prototypes, printed direct and then cut, creased or welded in our Brisbane factory. From one prototype to a national roll-out.

What we make

  • Counter and point-of-sale displays
  • Menu boards and table talkers
  • Shelf signs and wobblers
  • Product stands and dispensers
  • Window and door signs on clear sheet
  • Packaging prototypes and short-run boxes

Printed and made to shape

Displays often need more than a print. We print the sheet, then cut, crease and fold polypropylene, or weld PVC to make pockets, stands and holders, so the finished display comes from one factory.

Short runs for a store trial are as easy as a national roll-out.

Questions we get asked

Can you make a prototype first?

Yes. We can print and make a single display for sign-off before the full run.

Do you print on card for packaging mock-ups?

Yes. We print heavy card and board for packaging prototypes and short runs.

Can you fold and crease plastic displays?

Yes. We crease and fold polypropylene and weld PVC in house.

File checklist

  • PDF preferred, with fonts outlined or embedded.
  • Artwork at finished size, with 3 mm bleed for edge-to-edge prints.
  • Photos and raster images at 150 to 300 dpi at finished size.
  • CMYK colour. Tell us any brand colours that must match.
  • White ink on its own spot colour layer. We confirm the naming when we check your file.
