UV flatbed printing · Brisbane

Full-colour printed plastic products, made in Brisbane

We print full colour onto PVC and polypropylene sheet, then cut, weld, crease, foil and assemble it into finished folders, wallets, tags and covers, all in one Brisbane factory.

Full colour without screens

Screen printing suits long runs in a few spot colours. Flatbed UV printing puts full-colour artwork, photos and gradients onto PVC and polypropylene with no screens to make, so short runs and personalised items cost far less to set up.

We then turn the printed sheet into the finished product with the same equipment we have used for 27 years.

Products we print and make

  • Full-colour folders, binders and presentation covers
  • Printed wallets, pockets and document holders
  • Tags, cards, luggage tags and swing tags
  • Menu covers and hospitality holders
  • Music and school folders with photo artwork
  • Printed covers and panels for your own products

Print then make

Every step happens in our Tingalpa factory.

  1. Print

    Full colour and white printed flat onto PVC or polypropylene sheet.

  2. Cut

    Guillotined to size or die cut to shape.

  3. Form

    RF welded, creased and folded, or foil blocked.

  4. Fit and pack

    Ring mechanisms, eyelets, rivets and press studs fitted, then checked and packed.

Questions we get asked

When is UV printing better than screen printing?

For full-colour artwork, photos, gradients and short runs. Screen printing is still the better value for long runs in one or two spot colours. We do both and will recommend the right one.

Can you print each item differently?

Yes. Names, numbers or different artwork on each piece cost little extra with flatbed printing.

What is the minimum run?

There is no set minimum. We quote samples and short runs per job.
