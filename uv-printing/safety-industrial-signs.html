Questions we get asked

Can you match our site's sign standard?

Yes. Send your existing artwork or the standard your site follows and we print to it. We do not make compliance assessments for your site.

Can you print numbered labels and asset tags?

Yes. Send a spreadsheet of numbers or text and we print them as one batch.

Do you make lockout and danger tags?

Yes. Plastics Unique has made danger and out-of-service tags for years. We can print them full colour and punch them for ties.

File checklist

  • PDF preferred, with fonts outlined or embedded.
  • Artwork at finished size, with 3 mm bleed for edge-to-edge prints.
  • Photos and raster images at 150 to 300 dpi at finished size.
  • CMYK colour. Tell us any brand colours that must match.
  • White ink on its own spot colour layer. We confirm the naming when we check your file.

Not print-ready? Send what you have. We check every file before printing and tell you if anything needs fixing.
