Already a supplier to schools

Plastics Unique has made school folders, music folders, student portfolios and certificate pockets for Queensland schools for years. The same factory now prints signs, so your school can order folders and signs from one supplier with one account.

  • Your crest in full colour. Printed direct onto acrylic, PVC or ACM, with white ink on clear and dark boards.
  • Add names each year. Honour boards can be made as panels so new names are added without replacing the board.
  • Indoor or outdoor. ACM and aluminium for outdoor signs, acrylic and PVC indoors.

Questions we get asked

Can you add names to an honour board each year?

Yes. We can design the board as separate panels or strips so a new panel is printed each year and added to the board.

Do you supply signs to schools outside Brisbane?

Yes. We freight Australia-wide. Signs are packed flat and protected for transport.

Can we order on a school purchase order?

Yes. Send your purchase order with the quote acceptance.
